Shadow Manhua Uploader - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Shadow Manhua Uploader ("the app") is a private desktop tool that the Shadow Manhua channel (مانهوا الظل) uses to post its own episodes to its own TikTok, YouTube and Facebook accounts.

1. What the app accesses

Through TikTok Login Kit the app receives, for the channel's own TikTok account only: the account's open ID, display name and avatar (user.info.basic), and permission to upload and post videos to that account (video.upload, video.publish). It does not read other users' data, followers, messages or comments.

2. How it is used

The data is used only to post the channel's videos and to confirm which account is signed in.

3. Storage

The sign-in token is kept on the channel owner's own computer only. It is never sold, shared or sent anywhere other than the platform's own API.

4. Removal

Access can be withdrawn at any time in TikTok's settings (Security and permissions, Apps). The owner can delete the stored token from the computer at any time.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from them.
